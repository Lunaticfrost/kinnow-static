Swapping is simple and fast. If you see an upcoming meal you want to change, open the Kinnow app, navigate to the "My Meals" tab, tap "Swap" on order card, and select from the available swap options for that day. This allows you to customize your menu to fit your taste. Please ensure you make any swaps before the 11:00 PM cut-off time the night before.

If you're heading out, eating out, or just want to skip a day, tap the "Skip" button on your upcoming meal card in the app. There are no questions asked, no calls to make, and your subscription day rolls over automatically so you don't lose any money/order. Please ensure you submit your skip before the 11:00 PM cut-off time the night before.

To ensure freshness and avoid food wastage, the cut-off time for skips and swaps is 11:00 PM on the night before your scheduled delivery.

Instead of pausing, you can simply skip your meals for the days you are away. If you run out of skips, you can request additional skips on-demand by reaching out to our support team, and they will add them for you.
