Got Questions?

Bangalore Delivery FAQs

Which areas in Bangalore do you deliver to? +
We currently deliver across HSR Layout, Koramangala, Bellandur, Sarjapur Road, BTM Layout, and surrounding tech hubs. More areas like Indiranagar, Whitefield, and Electronic City are launching soon!
What time do morning deliveries happen? +
Deliveries take place every morning between 5:00 AM and 10:00 AM. You can select your exact preferred delivery slot when setting up your plan in the Kinnow app.
What if I am traveling out of Bangalore? +
No problem! You can pause or skip your subscription meals with a single click in the app before 11 PM the night before. Skipped days are automatically added back to your balance.
Is Kinnow food 100% vegetarian? +
Yes, 100%! All Kinnow meals are 100% pure vegetarian, prepared fresh in hyper-hygienic micro-kitchens with zero reheating and zero artificial preservatives.
